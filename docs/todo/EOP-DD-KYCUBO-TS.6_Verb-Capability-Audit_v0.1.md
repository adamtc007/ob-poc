## §1 The ruling: a pack is a capability

**Two packs, two manifests, two loaders, two hashes.** Not one pack with a field distinguishing its members — the separation is structural.

| Pack | Capability | Writes |
|---|---|---|
| **Assembly** | build the board of truth: gather facts, prove them, correct them | the fact stream |
| **Evaluation** | test the board against policy, and rule on the result | its own decision and run records — **never** the fact stream |

**Why this beats a `Capability` enum on `LexiconEntry`** (the v0.3 proposal, now withdrawn): a field must be set correctly and consulted by something. Two manifests need neither. The evaluation pack has **no write path to the fact stream by construction**, so purity is architectural rather than asserted — `assess_verbs_never_write` stops being a test and becomes a property of the build. This programme has found four defects of the form *the rule existed and nothing consulted it*; the way to not find a fifth is to make the rule unnecessary.

**Second-order benefit — the packs version independently.** Assembly is domain vocabulary that changes slowly: entity types, pipes, moves. Evaluation is policy that changes constantly: regulations, thresholds, jurisdictions. Bound into one manifest, every policy tweak moves the assembly hash and drifts open assembly sessions for nothing. Split, a determination pins the **assembly** pack version and a run book pins the **evaluation** pack version — the two-clocks model, made concrete.

## §2 The boundary

**Evaluation reads:** the constructed taxonomy at (T, axis, scope), its determination, its assurance profile, and their pins. **Evaluation never reads:** raw assertions. **Evaluation never writes:** the fact stream.

One direction, narrow surface. If a check ever needs a raw assertion, that is a signal the constructed board is missing something — fix the board, not the boundary.

The evaluation pack then *assesses that fact* and stays pure and reproducible. Without this split, one check reaches outside, becomes non-reproducible, and purity is lost for the sake of one verb.

## §6 Deferred: splitting the two assertion verbs

`assert-control` conflates asserted control — board appointment, trustee powers, GP designation, management mandate, contractual veto, each a documented fact — with **derived** control: voting rights, which falls out of the share register and the articles. Nobody hands over a document saying "A controls B". `assert-economic-interest` records a percentage where the facts handed over are shares issued by class, the rights each class carries, and holdings by class.

Both splits wait on the **share-class fact layer**: removing `VotingRights` before voting power is derivable would leave the system unable to express voting control at all.

**Kept visible:** until then, economic and voting percentages remain the same number — wrong in any structure with more than one share class, which is the norm in exactly the fund and holding structures this system exists to model. A known deferral, not an unnoticed defect.
