### Closing-claim enumeration rule (added 2026-08-24, D2.1 reconciliation corrective tranche Item 6)

A tranche's close may not assert a summary claim ("all N gates closed", "the tranche is done") without an itemized enumeration beneath it: **every gate NAMED in the ratified document being closed**, each marked exactly one of **BUILT** (cite the test function and file), **DEFERRED** (the existing four-field WHAT/WHY/WHO/WHEN rule, D2.1 §3), or **NOT DONE** (stated plainly, no euphemism).

**Why this rule exists, not restated obliquely:** the 2026-08-23 D2.1 reconciliation found the redefine-and-report-landed failure recurred TWICE inside the D2.1 tranche itself — `unevaluable_is_not_fail` and `work_list_is_derived_from_latest_run` were both marked "rehomed onto production" in `kyc_d21_gate_rehoming.rs`'s own header, when neither closing test in fact re-ran the same subject through a real board-state change to observe the property (see §5j Item 1 below). The existing four-field deferral rule held throughout — nothing was deferred without four fields. **What failed was the CLOSING CLAIM itself**: a prose summary asserting closure that no per-gate enumeration backed, so a false claim about two specific named gates hid inside an otherwise-true claim about the tranche as a whole. An enumeration is falsifiable in a way a summary sentence is not — a reviewer checks each named gate against its cited test in isolation, without reconstructing the tranche's history from chat.

**How to apply:** find the ratified document's own list of named gates (its "§6 Gate tests" section or equivalent). Build a table, one row per gate, in the document's own naming — no paraphrase substitutes for the document's own name. Every gate in the document's list appears exactly once. If the enumeration can't find a document's gate by name in the landed test suite, that absence is itself a finding, not something to silently reconcile away. See §5j for this rule's own first application, against D2.0 §6 (9 gates) and D2.1 §6 (9 gates).

## 1. The frame

**D1 — establish the facts.** Assemble the alleged set of entities and connections; prove what can be proved; construct the best possible taxonomy. **D2 — assess the facts.** Judge the taxonomy against regulation, BNY policy, legal barriers.

**D2 needs no completeness gate.** It may run against a board in any state; it simply fails, and *the failure is the work list*. That is why D1 comes first and why nothing in D1 knows policy exists. The interface is one-directional: D2 receives the taxonomy at (T, axis, scope) with its determination, assurance profile and pins, and never reaches back into raw assertions.

**Everything in this document is D1.**
