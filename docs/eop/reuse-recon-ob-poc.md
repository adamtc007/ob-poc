### 2.2 Grammar expressiveness for CA's constructs

Parser grammar: `atom ::= '(' symbol name? slot* ')'`, `slot ::= keyword value`, `value ::= atom | for-each | list | map | qualified-name | symbol | string | int | float | bool | slot-ref | template-subst | template-splice | insertion-marker` (`dsl-parser/src/parser.rs:11-21`). | construct | verdict | finding (file:line) | severity | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `(guard …)` clock constraint on a node/edge | **expressible by convention** as a slot value `:guard (guard :deadline …)`; **needs a grammar change** as a top-level atom | Nested atoms are accepted anywhere a value is (`parser.rs:396-410`); a top-level `guard` kind is rejected (`kinds.rs:95` + `atom_bag.rs:66-70`). ## 6. CA-needs-that-do-not-exist-here

1. **A generic legality enumerator** (current node × clock reached-set × role).
